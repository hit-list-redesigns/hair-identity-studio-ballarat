Your visit, step by step.

No surprises, from booking to goodbye.

  1. Your request is received

    When you book

    Then a confirmation, or another time offered.

  2. A few short forms

    Before your visit

    Welcome, hair history, and a sensory questionnaire if relevant.

  3. Confirm and update

    4 days before

    By email and SMS, with a chance to update preferences.

  4. Two gentle reminders

    The day before · 4 hours before

    Email the day before; SMS with the address 4 hours before.

  5. Come in through the side gate

    Arriving

    Arrive on time (early? wait in your car). Take a moment to settle.

  6. It starts with a conversation

    Every appointment

    Your goals and worries. No hair jargon needed.

  7. However feels right for you

    During

    Questions, breaks, headphones and comfort items all welcome.

  8. Pay at the end

    Finishing

    Cash or EFTPOS. Products and rebooking only if you want.
